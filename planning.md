# Problem 1: Electric Truck Route

## Prompt:
Suppose an electric truck carrying heavy material travels from Pittsburgh to Erie each morning along I-79 and only stops at charging stations to recharge its battery. The truck's battery has a range of exactly B miles, and the charging stations are always situated at mile markers. 

## Goal:
Design an algorithm to find the minimum number of stops necessary to reach Erie without running out of power.

## Input
- battery range B
- list of integers representing charging station locations
